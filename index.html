What's on the Panera Bread menu?

The Panera Bread menu is split into a handful of clear sections: breakfast (bagel stacks, egg sandwiches, soufflés and oatmeal), sandwiches (hot, toasted and cold), soups & mac, salads and grain bowls, Value Duets, the bakery (bagels, cookies, pastries and whole breads), a kids menu and drinks. Panera Bread salads

Panera salads are full meals, not side dishes. Chicken salads such as the Fuji Apple, Asian Sesame and Greek with Chicken cost about $11.99, the Harvest Medley and Southwest Chicken Ranch about $12.99, and the Green Goddess Chicken Cobb about $13.59. Meatless versions of the Caesar and Greek are about $8.99. Dressings, toppings and the full salad line-up are on the Panera Salads Menu page.

Panera Bread coffee and drinks

Panera's hot coffee comes in light roast, dark roast, hazelnut and decaf for about $2.99. Espresso drinks run from about $2.49 for a single espresso to $5.19 for a Caramel, Mocha or Crème Brûlée Latte, and iced versions cost the same. Lemonades, teas, frescas and smoothies round out the cooler. For all coffee sizes, seasonal lattes and cold brews, see the Panera Coffee Menu page.

Customising your Panera order

Most items on the Panera Bread menu can be changed. You can swap the bread on a sandwich, add or remove cheese, sauce and toppings, choose a side such as a baguette, chips, an apple or a fruit cup, and upgrade a soup to a bread bowl. The app shows each change and its price before you pay. Panera's long-running You Pick Two combo, half a sandwich or salad with a cup of soup or Mac & Cheese, is also available at many bakery-cafes. Its price varies, so check the app.

Ordering for a group

For meetings and parties, buying in bulk from the Panera Bread menu is easier than ordering one meal at a time. Soups come by the quart (about $21.99), bagels by the Baker's Dozen (about $13.99, or $9.99 on Tuesdays at many cafes), lemonades and teas by the half gallon (about $7.29–$9.49) and cookies by the dozen (about $22.49). Family Feasts start at about $36. The group order planner above turns your head count into a shopping list.

The Panera kids menu

Kids' meals include Grilled Cheese (about $5.79), Broccoli Cheddar Soup ($6.79), Mac & Cheese ($6.99) and Turkey or Ham & Cheese sandwiches ($7.19). Add an apple or a carton of milk for a balanced lunch.

Nutrition and allergens

Panera publishes calories, nutrition and allergen information for every item on its menu in the app, on its official website and in the bakery-cafe. If you have an allergy, check the details and talk to the team before you order. Recipes and suppliers can change.

Saving money on the Panera Bread menu

  1. Pick a Value Duet for a sandwich or salad and soup at about $7.99.
  2. Upgrade to a bread bowl for only about 59¢ more.
  3. Buy bagels on Tuesday for the Baker's Dozen deal.
  4. Join MyPanera, the free rewards program, for member offers.
  5. Try the Unlimited Sip Club if you buy a drink most weeks.
  6. Order ahead for pickup to skip delivery-app markups.

About these prices

Panera bakery-cafes are run by the company and by franchise partners, so prices change from one location to another. Airports and city centres usually cost more. The prices here are typical U.S. prices, last checked in November 2026.

FAQ

Panera Bread menu questions

What is the most popular item on the Panera Bread menu?

Broccoli Cheddar Soup, especially in a sourdough bread bowl (about $9.08), is the item most closely linked with Panera. The Bacon Turkey Bravo sandwich and the Cinnamon Crunch Bagel are also long-time favourites.

How much is a sandwich at Panera Bread?

Panera Bread sandwiches cost about $7.99 to $13.39 in 2026. The Grilled Cheese and Mediterranean Veggie are the cheapest at about $7.99, and the Ciabatta Cheesesteak is the most expensive at about $13.39.

Can you get half a sandwich at Panera?

Yes. Panera's You Pick Two combo lets you pair half a sandwich or salad with a cup of soup or Mac & Cheese at many bakery-cafes. Value Duets offer a similar pairing for about $7.99.

How much does Panera charge for a quart of soup?

A quart of most Panera soups costs about $21.99, enough for about four 8-oz cups. A quart of Mac & Cheese is about $25.99.

What drinks are on the Panera Bread menu?

Panera serves hot coffee, lattes and espresso, iced tea, lemonades, frescas, fountain sodas, smoothies, hot chocolate, milk and juice. Prices range from about $2.49 to $6.49.

Does Panera Bread have breakfast all day?

Breakfast items are a morning menu at most bakery-cafes, but bagels, pastries and many bakery items are sold all day. Check the app for your cafe.

Where can I see Panera nutrition and allergen information?

Panera publishes calories, nutrition and allergen details for every item in its app, on its official website and in the bakery-cafe.
